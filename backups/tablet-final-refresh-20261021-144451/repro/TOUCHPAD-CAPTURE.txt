DELL ELAN TOUCHPAD CAPTURE (Linux Live)

Boot the SanDisk with F12 > UEFI SanDisk > Dell sensor capture - Fedora Live.
Keep the laptop unfolded. In Terminal run:

sudo /bin/bash /run/initramfs/live/start-touchpad-capture.sh

Press Enter once when prompted. Follow five timed phases: one-finger movement,
two-finger scrolling, pinch, three-finger swipe, four-finger swipe. Each has
3 seconds to lift fingers followed by 8 seconds of movement. No typing is needed
while capturing. Xfce need not perform a desktop action for these gestures;
the collector checks raw touch contact delivery rather than desktop bindings.

Reports go to LATSENSORS/reports/touchpad-<timestamp>. Wait for SAVED, shut down
Linux, then return the SanDisk to the Mac. Existing sensor reports are preserved.

Includes: exact ELAN 04f3:328e I2C HID descriptor and physical ancestry, absolute
ranges/slot capacity, firmware tables, GPIO/pinctrl metadata, IRQ counters and
kernel transport errors before/after, and per-gesture frame/contact/drop metrics.
Delivery gaps include finger lifts and pauses and are not WindowServer frame
latency. No keyboard events, pointer-coordinate traces or tracking IDs are saved.
Only the selected ELAN touchpad event node is opened, its identity is rechecked,
and no EVIOCGRAB or driver/power configuration change is made. Read-only debugfs
may be mounted to expose GPIO diagnostics. No packages or OS installation needed.

This is a diagnostic collector, not a macOS touchpad driver or a gesture fix.
